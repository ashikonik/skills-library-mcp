# Platform reference

## Character limits

| Platform | Limit | Link cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bluesky | 300 | 23 chars | Facets for mentions |
| Mastodon | 500 | 23 chars | Hashtags only in `tags` |
| LinkedIn | 3000 | ~100 chars | First 2 lines before "see more" |
| Reddit | 40000 body, 300 title | — | Per-subreddit rules |
| Instagram | 2200 | — | First 125 chars before truncation |
| TikTok | 4000 | — | Effectively unused |

## Character budget

| Platform | Body | Hashtags | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bluesky | 250 | 0-3 | 1 |
| Mastodon | 450 | 0-3 | 1 |
| LinkedIn | 1500 | 3-5 | 1 |
| Reddit | 2500 | 0 | 1 |

## Hashtag placement

| Platform | Where | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Bluesky | facets | 0-3 |
| Mastodon | `tags` field, never the body | 0-3 |
| LinkedIn | end of the body | 3-5 |
| Instagram | end of the caption | 3-8 |
| TikTok | end of the caption | 3-8 |
| Reddit | nowhere | 0 |

## Content pillar rotation

| Pillar | Share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Education | 40% | Teach something useful |
| Proof | 20% | Results, case studies |
| Story | 20% | Behind the scenes |
| Offer | 20% | The ask |

Never three offer posts in a row.

## Scheduling rules

- Store timestamps in UTC
- Minimum 2 hours between posts on one platform
- Post frequency consistent beats frequent
- Vary the hook per platform even when the body is shared
